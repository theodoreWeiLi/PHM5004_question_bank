### 1. Microarray Applications

Question:&#x20;
Microarray can be used to study which of the following?

Options:&#x20;
A. Genetic polymorphism
B. Transcriptome
C. Proteome
D. Genetic polymorphism and transcriptome only
E. All of the above ✅

Analysis:&#x20;
Microarrays are primarily used for detecting gene expression (transcriptome) and genetic variants (polymorphisms). They are not suitable for proteome analysis.

### 2. SNP Detection Techniques

Question:&#x20;
What is a fundamental difference between allele-specific hybridization and allele-specific extension in SNP detection?

Options:&#x20;
A. Both use probes for SNP distinction
B. Hybridization requires exact probe matching; extension relies on polymerase fidelity ✅
C. Extension uses probe binding; hybridization uses enzymatic activity
D. Both methods involve DNA strand extension
E. Hybridization uses fluorescence; extension uses electrophoresis

Analysis:&#x20;
Hybridization depends on precise probe-target matching, while extension uses DNA polymerase to extend only if the base matches—highlighting fidelity differences.

### 15. Dominant Allele Frequency (q² = 0.16)

Question:&#x20;
In a population, 16% of individuals show a recessive phenotype. Assuming Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium, what is the frequency of the dominant allele?

Options:&#x20;
A. 0.2
B. 0.4
C. 0.6 ✅
D. 0.8
E. None of the Above
F. Don't Know

Analysis:&#x20;
q² = 0.16 → q = 0.4 → p = 1 − q = 0.6

### 16. Carrier Frequency (q² = 0.0001)

Question:&#x20;
A population is being studied for a disease caused by a recessive allele. The disease frequency is 1 in 10,000. What is the approximate frequency of carriers?

Options:&#x20;
A. 0.01 or 1 in 100
B. 0.02 or 1 in 50 ✅
C. 0.05 or 1 in 20
D. 0.001 or 1 in 1000
E. 0.0002 or 1 in 5000
F. None of the Above
G. Don't Know

Analysis:&#x20;
q² = 0.0001 → q = 0.01 → p = 0.99 → 2pq ≈ 0.02
